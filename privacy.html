Your privacy matters to us. This policy covers the information you share with LVS Mall through this website.

1. Who we are

This website is run by LVS Mall, New Airport Road, Chikkajala, Bengaluru 562157, a Lakshmi Enterprises property ("LVS Mall", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the forms on this website:

  • Contact form: your name, email address and, if you add them, your phone number and company, plus the topic and message you send.
  • Business enquiry form: the same details, and the type of business enquiry.
  • Feedback form: your ratings and comments about your visit, the date and stores you visited, and, only if you add them, your name, email address and phone number.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address.

We do not ask for payment details, we do not run user accounts, and we do not use this website to track you across other sites.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your message or enquiry.
  • To review visitor feedback and improve the mall, and to follow up if you ask us to.
  • To discuss advertising, collaboration, leasing or partnership opportunities you ask about.
  • To send you mall updates, if you signed up for the newsletter.
  • To keep records of enquiries and protect the website from spam and misuse.

4. Services that process your information

Our forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party service that emails your submission to the LVS Mall office and keeps a copy for up to 30 days. To block spam, the forms use Google reCAPTCHA, which may collect information about your device and browser under Google's own privacy policy.

When you open our location in Google Maps or visit our Instagram page, those services handle your information under their own policies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above so the website can work, or where we are required to by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to respond and for reasonable business records. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent to us using it. To do any of these, send us a message through our Contact page.

For any complaint about how we handle your information, write to us through our Contact page and choose "General enquiry". Our office will respond to you.

8. Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Google reCAPTCHA may use cookies to tell people apart from bots when you submit a form.

9. Children

This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive personal information through our forms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
