About LVS Mall

An open-air lifestyle destination on New Airport Road

LVS Mall sits on New Airport Road at Chikkajala, Bengaluru — right on the route between the city and Kempegowda International Airport. Every month, 50,000 to 1,00,000 shoppers — travellers, families and locals — visit the mall.

The mall brings together 32 well-known brands across fashion, sport, footwear, ethnic wear, home and food — from Nike, adidas and Levi's to Louis Philippe, Fabindia and Miniso, with a McDonald's drive-thru. Stores are open-air and easy to reach, most across two floors, with parking right outside every storefront.

Open 10 AM to 10 PM every day, with 24-hour on-site security and an interactive touch map to help shoppers find their way.
